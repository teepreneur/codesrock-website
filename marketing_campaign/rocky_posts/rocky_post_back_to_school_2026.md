As the 2026 school term kicks off, academic directors and parents share a common goal: preparing young learners (ages 3–8) with foundational computational thinking and problem-solving skills needed for an AI-driven future. 🧠💻

However, introducing coding in early grades often comes with challenges:
❌ High costs of building and maintaining computer labs.
