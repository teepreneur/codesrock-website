## 📄 Material 3: Teacher Training & Onboarding Brief
**Title:** 45-Minute Classroom Guide: Leading a CodesRock Screen-Free Robotics Session  
**Target Audience:** Early Childhood Teachers & STEM Club Instructors  

### Lesson Structure (45 Minutes):
1. **00–10 Mins | Storybook Teaser & Concept Introduction:** Read the comic episode from the CodesRock Activity Workbook. Introduce the day's challenge (e.g., *“Help Rocky build a obstacle-avoiding bot!”*).
2. **10–25 Mins | Hands-On Building:** Children assemble their robot using patented bolts/nuts (Mechanics Kit) or rivets (Robotics Kit). Teachers guide hand-eye coordination.
3. **25–38 Mins | Physical Coding & Debugging:**
   - *Mechanics Kit:* Children place physical NFC command blocks on the desktop board and press START.
   - *Robotics Kit:* Children use the Optical Smart Coding Pen to tap coding cards in sequence.
4. **38–45 Mins | Showcase & Reflection:** Robots move, light up, and play melodies on the classroom mat!
