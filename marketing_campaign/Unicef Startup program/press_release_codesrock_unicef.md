## CodesRock Labs Selected for UNICEF Startup Lab Cohort 6, Poised to Transform Early Childhood STEM Education Across Africa with Screen-Free AI, Robotics & Coding Program

*Ghana-based EdTech startup brings tactile, AI-powered learning to children as young as 3—without a single screen*

---

**ACCRA, GHANA — [Date, 2026]** — CodesRock Labs, Ghana's pioneering screen-free computational thinking program for children aged 3 to 8, today announced its selection as a member of the **UNICEF Startup Lab Cohort 6**—a prestigious accelerator that supports mission-driven startups dedicated to improving children's lives across the globe.

The recognition validates CodesRock's innovative approach to bridging the 21st-century skills gap in African early childhood education: equipping children with the foundational logic, creativity, and problem-solving abilities of the AI and robotics era—without relying on tablets, laptops, or screens.

---

### About CodesRock Labs

CodesRock Labs was founded on a single conviction: that every African child deserves to speak the language of the future, regardless of their access to technology. The program replaces screen-based coding curricula with a comprehensive **physical learning ecosystem** comprising:

- **RockBot** — A friendly, programmable robot that children guide using tactile command cards
- **Adinkra-Themed Coding Mats** — Culturally grounded sequencing and algorithmic thinking activities
- **Activity Books & Logic Flashcards** — Hands-on exercises that build pattern recognition and computational thinking
- **Songs & Audio Learning Guides** — Multisensory reinforcement for core coding concepts
- **Teacher Portal** — An open-source digital platform providing lesson guides, assessment tools, and professional development resources for educators

The program serves children in **Nursery, Kindergarten, and early Primary School**, and is currently operational in **9 partner schools**, having directly impacted over **317 children**, with 68% of those reached from low-income communities. Schools partnered with CodesRock have reported a **+200% improvement** in pupils' problem-solving skills within one term of adoption.

### The Problem CodesRock Solves

In Sub-Saharan Africa, the majority of preschools and kindergartens lack the infrastructure to run computer-based coding curricula. Most schools delay STEM instruction until Junior High—a critical missed window, as research consistently shows that computational thinking skills are most effectively developed between ages 3 and 8.

Traditional solutions require expensive computer labs, reliable electricity, and trained ICT teachers. CodesRock eliminates all three barriers with a fully offline, physical-first, teacher-friendly system that any educator can deploy on Day 1 with minimal training.

### Teacher Portal: A Digital Public Good

In conjunction with the UNICEF Startup Lab cohort activities, CodesRock is launching its **open-source Teacher Portal**—a Digital Public Good providing:

- Structured lesson plans aligned to early childhood computational thinking standards
- Professional development video guides for teachers
- Assessment and progress tracking tools
- A community hub for school-based educators to share best practices

The Teacher Portal underscores CodesRock's commitment to equitable access: institutions can access the foundational curriculum layer at no cost, removing financial barriers to adoption.
